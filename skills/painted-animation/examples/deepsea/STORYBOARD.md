# 深海 · 大肥鱼 — "The last deep-sea light" (14 s, 16:9, 24 fps, 112 BPM)

**Logline:** 大肥鱼 wants to see what is glowing on the seabed; the light is a tiny fish trapped in a shell, so she
chomps the shell open and lets it ride on her headdress up to the surface.

**World:** one ocean, one cool palette that warms as she rises: the abyss (deep indigo, cyan rays, marine snow) → the
shell's warm gold glow → the pale aqua and gold of the surface. Light comes from above and from the shell.

**Motif:** the little fish. It starts as a glow inside the shell, swims free, circles her whale tail, and settles on her
headdress. The ending rhymes with the opening: the camera closes on the same two lights it opened on, now together.

**Her arc:** happy (gliding down) → surprised (notices the light) → idea → chomp (effort) → surprised (it is alive) →
love → laugh → happy (rising) → proud.

**Lessons it taught the skill:** the whale tail needs its own beat even when she is still; a chibi's head lags the body
on every start; a glow needs a warm thing to sit in or it reads as fog; put the second character's entrance on its own
beat so the viewer has time to find it.
