**Emphasis / time budget**: this template is an 18-second *demonstration* of the skill, not a portfolio reel. The lead
idea (the mark and the name) gets 9 s of the 18; the mechanism shot and the principle get 3 s each. When you adapt it,

**Source of truth for on-screen facts**: none here — every word is a slogan or a label, nothing is a statistic. When you

**Thread / device**: one dot of light in the deep. It becomes the fish mark, the mark becomes the name, the name sends a
request through the deep, the answer dissolves into the current, and the reel collapses back to the dot. The palette
runs abyss → deep blue → surface aqua → foam, one step brighter per chapter.
